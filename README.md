# 🌍 TAB HISTORY

### **EXPLORE • DISCOVER • LEARN**

**TAB HISTORY** is an educational platform designed for students to explore the amazing world of **Birds, Animals, Trees, and Plants**.

Discover beautiful photographs, names, scientific information, habitats, history, interesting facts, and the importance of living things in our natural world.

---

## 📚 About the Project

TAB HISTORY works like a digital encyclopedia of the natural world.

Students can:

* 🐦 Explore birds from around the world
* 🦁 Discover animals and their habitats
* 🌳 Learn about different trees
* 🌿 Explore plants and their uses
* 📖 Read history and interesting facts
* 🔍 Search for species by name
* 🌎 Explore species by continent and habitat
* 🧠 Take educational quizzes
* ⚖️ Compare different species
* 💡 Discover random "Did You Know?" facts

---

## 🌎 Main Categories

### 🐦 Birds

Explore birds from different regions and habitats, including their names, photographs, scientific names, diets, habitats, lifespans, history, and conservation status.

### 🦁 Animals

Discover mammals, reptiles, amphibians, aquatic animals, and other fascinating species from around the world.

### 🌳 Trees

Learn about trees such as Banyan, Neem, Oak, Maple, Redwood, Baobab, Eucalyptus, Coconut, and many more.

### 🌿 Plants

Explore flowers, herbs, crops, medicinal plants, aquatic plants, and other plant species.

The goal of TAB HISTORY is to make learning about nature **simple, visual, interactive, and interesting for students**.
